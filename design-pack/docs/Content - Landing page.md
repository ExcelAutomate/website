## Carousel — "What modern Excel can do"

Five cards, paged with the arrows or the dots beneath. Cards alternate green and near-black backgrounds. Keep the body text to a similar length across cards — they share a fixed height, so a much longer card will look cramped and a much shorter one leaves the peek edge blank.

### Card 1 — Excel

Modern Excel is more than just spreadsheets and formulas. Under the hood is a powerful platform for processing your data, a bridge that can bring your systems together, and a window that lets you see into it all. With a little bit of help it can become the right solution rather than part of the problem.

### Card 2 — Automate

Turn hours of data wrangling into the click of a few buttons, so you can focus on the things you do best. Let Excel take over the repetitive tasks, with efficient, robust, repeatable processes.

### Card 3 — Integrate

Bring together data from all your different platforms and sources - cloud based systems, CSV exports, databases, PDFs, other Excel spreadsheets and more. Combine, compare, and re-export with just a few clicks, or turn it into your own reporting dashboard.

### Card 4 — Accelerate

Speed up your processes and enable greater responsiveness - get work done when it's needed, without it disrupting your day. And with rapid development in Modern Excel, you can have custom tools built and ready to use faster than ever.

### Card 5 — Transform

Transform your data, transform your processes, and transform the way you use Excel.

## Services — "How Excel Automate can help"

**Section heading:** How Excel Automate can help

Six tiles, each with an icon. Titles are sentence case - only "Power Query" keeps its capitals, as a product name. Tiles are laid out in a grid, so an odd number leaves a gap — keep the count even (six or eight) unless the layout changes.

### 1. Power Query automation
*Icon: funnel*

Fast and powerful data integration and transformations - the best way to combine data from multiple sources and prepare it for importing, reporting, migrating and more.

### 2. VBA automation
*Icon: brackets*

The full featured programming language that sits behind Excel - automate everything that isn't just data tables, including forms, files, formatting and more, or even running other programs from Excel.

### 3. Data cleansing
*Icon: check*

Identify duplicates and inconsistencies, standardise formatting, fix or remove bad data, and prepare a file ready to import back into your system.

### 4. Data migration
*Icon: migrate*

Take the data from your existing system and prepare it for import into a new one - all in a repeatable process that can be used for testing, initial import, and final cutover.

### 5. Reports and dashboards
*Icon: chart*

Take the cleaned and consolidated data from Power Query, and distil it down to the figures and charts that tell you what you need to know.

### 6. System design
*Icon: network*

Find the best way to combine your existing data platforms, your in-house talent, and the full Excel toolkit to streamline your tasks.
