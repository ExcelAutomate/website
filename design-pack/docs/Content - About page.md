## Where I'm coming from

**Heading:** Where I'm coming from

> A lot of my recent work has been with non-profit organisations, where money is better spent on doing good than on sophisticated systems and large IT teams. The downside I've seen is that a lot of tasks get stuck being done the slow manual way, which has its own costs over time.

> With a business focused on finding fast and efficient custom solutions - something that **Modern Excel** is perfect for - I'm aiming to help more organisations across Australia save time, costs, and headaches.

> Being based in Hobart, I'm also keen to work with any businesses in Tasmania who might be dealing with similar issues.

---

## What I'm about

**Heading:** What I'm about

> I always enjoy solving problems, but the part of this work I like best isn't the code and the data. It's working with the **people** who use the systems day in day out, who labour over data and wrestle with spreadsheets.

> Not everyone has time to become an expert at Excel, and if I can do my part to make the job easier, they can focus on all the other things they do to bring value.
