AI tools are good at the parts of spreadsheet work that are fiddly rather than difficult: explaining a formula somebody else wrote, suggesting an approach, drafting a first attempt at a transformation, or naming things consistently.

They are much less reliable at anything requiring certainty about your actual data. A confident wrong answer about a number is worse than no answer, because it looks finished.

Our position is that these tools belong in the building, not in the running. Using them to help write a query is sensible. Having them decide what the numbers mean, on a report somebody acts on, is not.
