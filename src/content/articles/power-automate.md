Power Automate handles the joins: an email arrives with an attachment, the attachment lands in the right folder, a query refreshes, somebody gets notified. Each step is unremarkable, and stringing them together removes a surprising amount of routine work.

It is at its best on the boundaries between systems, where nobody owns the handover and it gets done manually by whoever notices. Those are the tasks most likely to be forgotten under pressure.

The caution is that a long chain of automated steps can be hard to follow when it breaks. We keep flows short, make failures visible, and write down what each one is for.
