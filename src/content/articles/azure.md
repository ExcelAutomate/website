Azure is Microsoft's cloud platform, and for our purposes that mostly means somewhere to put a database or a scheduled job that is not somebody's desktop.

The problem it solves is availability. A refresh that runs on a machine under a desk stops working when that machine is off, or when the person who set it up leaves. A hosted one keeps going.

It is also where the costs need watching most carefully, because it is billed by consumption. Part of the job is sizing it honestly and saying when the answer is a shared folder instead.
