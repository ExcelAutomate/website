Power BI is built for one job: presenting data to people who are not going to edit it. Interactive charts, filters that apply everywhere at once, and a report that refreshes itself on a schedule and looks the same for everybody.

It shares a great deal with Modern Excel, which is what makes it a natural next step rather than a fresh start. Power Query is the same tool, and the modelling ideas carry across directly.

It is not free, and it is not always warranted. If three people look at a report monthly and want to poke at the underlying numbers, Excel is genuinely better. Board reporting, or a figure a dozen people check weekly, is where Power BI starts paying for itself.
