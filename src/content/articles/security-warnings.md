If we have built you something with macros in it, you will meet the security bar, and possibly a more alarming red one. Knowing what they mean makes the difference between using the tool and being afraid of it.

The article will explain the difference between the ordinary yellow trust bar and the blocked-file message that appears on downloads, and why a file emailed to you behaves differently from the same file on a network drive.

It will also cover the safe way to unblock a file you are expecting, trusted locations as an alternative to enabling content every time, and the one situation where you should not click enable: a workbook you were not expecting, from someone you cannot verify.
