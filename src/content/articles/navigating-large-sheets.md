Scrolling is the slowest way to move around a spreadsheet, and on a sheet with fifty thousand rows it is close to useless. There are half a dozen keyboard moves that replace nearly all of it.

The article will cover jumping to the edges of a region, selecting to the end of a column without dragging, using the name box to go straight to a reference, and moving between sheets without the mouse.

Then keeping your place: freezing panes properly, splitting a window to see two parts of one sheet at once, and opening the same workbook in two windows side by side.
