A database handles the things a spreadsheet struggles with: many people writing at once, rules about what counts as valid data, a reliable record of what changed and when, and volumes that make Excel unhappy.

It does not replace Excel. In most of the setups we build, the database holds the data and Excel remains the place people look at it, with Power Query pulling straight from the source.

The article will also cover the modest end of this: a small database on a single server, or even a file-based one, is often enough. Not every improvement needs to be an enterprise system.
