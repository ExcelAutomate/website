# Visual checklist

Run through this in a browser after any change. No terminal, no devtools needed.
Look at what's described and confirm it matches "correct". Widen and narrow your
browser window for the items that mention it — a phone or a resized desktop window
both work.

## Every page

- [ ] Header: logo + wordmark + tagline on the left, four nav links, a green "Get in
      touch" button on the right (except the Contact page — see below).
- [ ] Narrow the window below about three-quarters of a normal laptop width: the nav
      links disappear and a burger icon (three lines) appears instead. Tap it — a menu
      drops down with all four links plus "Get in touch".
- [ ] The current page's nav link is underlined in green (Home / Our approach / For
      non-profits / About). The Contact page has no underlined item — it isn't in the
      nav.
- [ ] Scrolling down, each section fades and slides gently into place rather than
      appearing instantly.
- [ ] Footer at the very bottom: "© [this year] Excel Automate · ABN 51 680 585 107 ·
      Hobart, Tasmania".

## Landing page

- [ ] Big heading: "Make Excel **work** for you", with "work" in green, and an
      animated gear/grid graphic beside it that keeps turning slowly.
- [ ] Below the heading, a row of cards ("Excel", "Automate", "Integrate",
      "Accelerate", "Transform") that you can page through with the arrows or the dots
      underneath. The left ("back") arrow is invisible on the very first card and
      appears once you've moved forward.
- [ ] Six white tiles under "How Excel Automate can help", each with a small green icon.
- [ ] A green "Get in touch" band, then a dark "Learn more" band, in that order.

## Our approach

- [ ] A white card down the left side lists "Key ideas", "Modern Excel", "Excel tips",
      "Beyond Excel" — scrolling down highlights whichever section you're currently
      passing.
- [ ] The first green panel ("Key ideas") has three tiles, each ending in "Read this →".
      Clicking one opens that article as its own page; the "← Key ideas" link at the
      top takes you back.
- [ ] The three panels below Key ideas look slightly faded/washed out — that's
      deliberate (their articles are placeholder for now), and their tiles are still
      clickable.

## For non-profits

- [ ] Reads top to bottom as plain sections — no side menu, no card grid.
- [ ] Ends with a green "Get in touch" band, then a dark "Learn more" band.

## About

- [ ] A real photo of Chris sits to the right of the introduction on a wide screen, and
      moves below the text on a narrow one.
- [ ] The line "And that's a pretty good outcome." is set apart in italics with a green
      line beside it.
- [ ] Ends with a green "Get in touch" band, then a dark "Learn more" band.

## Contact

- [ ] The header's "Get in touch" is a flat green tag here, not a clickable button —
      you're already on this page.
- [ ] Choosing "Phone" under "Preferred reply" reveals a Phone field; the Email field
      stays visible either way.
- [ ] Leaving Name, Email or the message blank and pressing "Send message" shows a
      message next to the empty field(s) instead of sending.
- [ ] Filling in Name, Email and a message and sending shows a "Thanks, that's on its
      way" confirmation in the same card.
- [ ] Only one "Learn more" band at the bottom (no green band — the form is the ask on
      this page).
